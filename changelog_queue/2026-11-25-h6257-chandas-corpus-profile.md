- **feat(chandas): first metrical pass over DCS — guru/laghu meter detector, 272-stanza validation, corpus meter distribution by text and epoch (H6257)**
  **First metrical work in the estate.** [`scripts/chandas_meter.py`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-observatory/blob/main/scripts/chandas_meter.py) syllabifies the DCS CoNLL-U `# text` lines (IAST, digraph-aware phoneme clusters, `ai`/`au` diphthongs), marks each syllable guru/laghu by the classical rules (long/diphthong and closed syllables guru, `e`/`o` bha-guru, single medial onset laghu), groups lines into DCS `sent_counter` stanzas, and classifies by syllable-count signature against a pinned vṛtta table (24 gāyatrī, 30 uṣṇih-class, 32 anuṣṭubh/śloka, 44 triṣṭubh, 52 jagatī, 68 17×4 mandākrānta/indravajrā). Validated on **272 hand-sourced labelled stanzas** (`--validate`, text-level weak labels: Meghadūta = mandākrānta, Rāmāyaṇa = anuṣṭubh): precision 1.000 on both classes, recall 0.918/0.947, F1 0.957/0.973, zero cross-class confusions — all 18 errors are ±1 scanner residuals falling to `unclassified`. Canary: 20 stanzas labelled by hand in [`data/chandas_canary_manual_2026-11-16.tsv`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-observatory/blob/main/data/chandas_canary_manual_2026-11-16.tsv), detector agrees 19/20 (gate ≥18; the miss is a DCS 1.5-verse grouping anomaly). Corpus run (`--corpus`) over all 270 DCS texts (16 s): **94 poetic-layer texts (≥50 stanzas, ≥50% classified), 199,470 classified stanzas** — 95.6% anuṣṭubh/śloka, 3.8% triṣṭubh, 0.9% everything else — dataset at [`data/chandas_meter_distribution_2026-11-16.tsv`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-observatory/blob/main/data/chandas_meter_distribution_2026-11-16.tsv) with a hand-encoded conventional-dating epoch map ([`data/chandas_text_epochs_2026-11-16.tsv`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-observatory/blob/main/data/chandas_text_epochs_2026-11-16.tsv); triṣṭubh and the 17-syllable kāvya metres concentrate in the classical bucket, the medieval bucket is śloka-dominated purāṇa/nighaṇṭu verse). Full method, validation table and limits in [`reports/chandas_corpus_profile_2026-11-16.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-observatory/blob/main/reports/chandas_corpus_profile_2026-11-16.md). Declared limits (filed as GTD residuals): pattern-level disambiguation (mandākrānta vs indravajrā at 68, triṣṭubh variants) and the āryā mātrā-chandas family are v2; Vedic texts without `sent_counter` are line-level and undercounted. Offline test `tests/test_chandas_meter.py` pins the scanner selftest + prose negative control; ruff clean at baseline; `--selftest` PASS (7 checks), `--validate`/`--canary`/`--corpus` all exit 0.
