- analysis: H6049 Devanagari token penalty on our corpora — replication of arXiv:2609.12960's scheme-penalty methodology (penalty vs SLP1, bootstrap 95% CIs B=2000 + paired sign-flip permutation B=10000, char-ratio × tokens-per-char factorisation) on MW dictionary articles (Sanskrit layer, n=9,438 stride-sampled from 160,444 eligible entries, read from local csl-orig, not committed) and DCS 2026 prose sentences (n=1,961, five kathā/prose texts, restricted sqlite — only aggregates committed). Five tokenizer arms: GPT o200k (200,019 ids), GPT cl100k (100,277), LLaMA-3 (128,000, unsloth/llama-3.2-3b-instruct), GLM-4 (151,329, zai-org/glm-4-9b-chat-hf), ByT5 (byte-level), plus the derived TransLIST-style transliterate-then-tokenize arm.
