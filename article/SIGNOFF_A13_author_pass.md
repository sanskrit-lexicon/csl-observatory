## 2. Substance flags carried (not fixed)

1. Internal identifiers a journal reader cannot resolve: `A61`, `a61-history-v1.0`, `OBS-T`, `OBS-Q`, "the shared milestone layer", `evidence_pending`. A61 is cited as if it were a paper but has no reference-list entry; it should be cited by title as the companion paper (in preparation), and the OBS codes either expanded at first use or dropped.
2. Verbatim private correspondence is still quoted: two 2005 letters by Thomas Malten (one addressed to a third party), a 2013 personal email, and Scott Rhodes from the yearly volunteer call. Review item 2 of 02-07-2026 (permission or paraphrase) is unresolved.
3. Sharp passages are still present: Poona "as a *kūpamaṃḍūka*" and "there is no support for digitization to come from Poona"; "Baroda is out of the game"; the Manipal project "is discontinued" (unverified in the text); derived sites "some just a knockoff"; Acknowledgements "University of Cologne, which is otherwise nowadays uninvolved". Review item 3 is unresolved; a human should decide keep or soften.
4. The 41.6 % figure is still attached to two differently worded bases: "Out of 485 citation sources in MW, 330 sources are identical with PWG. 41.6% of citations link to the same sources" (330/485 is not 41.6 %, so the percentage must be a share of citation tokens, not of sources) and "If counted, it's 41.6% of cases" in the semi-digitization section. One definition should be stated once.
5. "(Gasūns 2024)" is cited for the MW/PWG lemma comparison (168633 vs 106169 lemmas, 87091 in common) but has no reference-list entry; only the presentations footnote lists 2024 talks. "Digital South Asia Library 1999" is in the reference list but never cited in the text.
6. The Apte 1957 public-domain argument (Gode 1961, Joshi 1963, sixty-year term ending 2023) rests on "Peter Scharf wrote recently" and "Thomas Malten has confirmed" with no document cited; it underwrites the statement that AP is served publicly at Cologne from 2025.
7. Third-person self-reference survives inside a first-person article: "Dr. Mārcis Gasūns (Russia) has been communicating via emails", "Gasūns' server", "not only for Dr. Gasūns", "the Reverse Sanskrit Dictionary Gasūns plans". Left untouched because it is body text, not a seam; a human should decide whether to convert these to "I".
8. Affiliation: the paper carries "Sanskrit Zealots' Society"; the brief's canonical byline says "independent scholar". Kept the Society; a human should decide which the venue carries.
9. The abstract dates its inventory to "June 2026" and OBS-Q to "2019 to 2026"; these must match the frozen A61 release the abstract names, which this pass did not verify.
10. Spelling is mixed between US ("organizational", "normalized", "digitization") and UK ("externalise", "pseudonymised", "labour") forms, mostly along the 2025-narrative / 2026-reframe boundary. A copy-edit item; the pass followed whichever form each passage already used.
11. The two conference-paper entries (Kretov, Gasūns and Leonchenko 2021 and 2022) and Patel 2016 have no proceedings or page data; the reference list still needs the IIJ form from review item B.
12. Proper-name fix in footnote 37: "Jim Fundeburk" → "Jim Funderburk". Kept because the article spells the name "Funderburk" everywhere else, but a personal name is not a voice matter; a human should confirm the spelling.
